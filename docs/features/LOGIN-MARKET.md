# Login exchange reminder

The login view places market information **outside the form card**. At desktop widths, a small navy panel sits beside the login card and shows concise exchange status, WIB clock and date. At widths up to 900px, the panel becomes a compact bar above the login card (about 59px at 320px), with date/footer omitted. The old top/bottom symbol ticker bands and their generation code are retired. The form keeps its own scroll area; both the board and the card fit within the viewport. Long session paragraphs remain hidden in login; full explanations remain on the dashboard. The concise status has its own polite live region. Verified at 320px and 1100px with no horizontal overflow.

The login card shows a friendly BEI reminder based on the existing `idxSession()` schedule and `Asia/Jakarta` time. Regular session I/II use a green status and encouragement; lunch, preopening, closing phases, weekends, and configured 2026 exchange holidays use their own messages. After the final session, the copy reads “Mau lihat apa? Bursa sedang tutup”. Users can always sign in; crypto remains available 24 hours.

This is a **scheduled reminder**, not an exchange connectivity feed or an indicator of trading halts for individual stocks. It reuses the asset-card schedule and holiday list. A year outside the configured calendar is explicitly marked as missing holiday coverage. Source: https://www.idx.id/id/produk-layanan/jam-dan-mekanisme-perdagangan

The UI refreshes every 15 seconds while visible and immediately when focus/visibility returns. It never fetches account data or submits the login form. Announcements update only when their text changes; the clock is outside the live region, and the small status animation respects reduced-motion settings. The existing scrolling login card accommodates smaller screens.

The dashboard has a separate market notice using the same schedule and wording as login. A 15-minute heads-up precedes lunch; lunch messaging explains the temporary pause and the session-II restart time (13:30 Monday–Thursday, 14:00 Friday, WIB). After the closing phases end at 16:15, messaging explains that displayed equity prices are last-known quotes. Equity cards, portfolio rows, and stock transaction rows have current market-session badges. These badges describe the current exchange schedule, not the execution status of a historical transaction. Crypto retains its 24-hour badge, and gold is not marked as closed by BEI hours. Session changes update views and reopening requests stock prices automatically. Market notices refresh on tab focus/visibility return as well as the 15-second timer.

Run `node scripts/tests/test-login-market.mjs` for Jakarta time, phase boundaries, Friday lunch, weekends, holidays, unknown calendar years, update hooks, and preservation of login controls. Run `node scripts/tests/test-portfolio-summary.mjs` for portfolio regressions.
